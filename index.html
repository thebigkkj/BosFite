BROCCOLI

BOSS FIGHT 01 — shapes & beats. move with WASD / arrows on PC, or just drag your finger on touch — the ship follows wherever you press. tap DASH / hit Spacebar to blink through hazards with full invincibility (half-second cooldown between dashes). watch for the highlighted zone — that's where the next hit lands. broccoli wanders the arena, one solid green column grows in partway through to block off part of the map, and the four corners are permanently cut off — no hiding in a pocket. patterns include rings, aimed spreads, floret drops, wall sweeps, spinning spirals, laser beams, and cross bursts — none of them track you, so it's all about reading and reacting. the lower its HP, the nastier it gets. there's a checkpoint at 50% HP — die after that and you resume from there instead of the top. 3 hits and you're steamed.
